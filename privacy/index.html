Privacy Policy

MI Party Prints Publishing
Effective date: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MI Party Prints Publishing, an internal tool operated by MI Party Prints, handles information in connection with its Pinterest publishing and content-management functions.

Purpose of the integration

MI Party Prints Publishing is used internally by MI Party Prints to manage its own Pinterest Business account. Its functions may include publishing owner-approved organic Pins to selected boards, reading information needed to manage those Pins and boards, and retrieving organic performance information for business analytics and content improvement.

Information accessed

When the Pinterest account is authorized, the integration may access information made available through the Pinterest API within the permissions granted by the account owner. This may include Pinterest account information, boards, Pins, Pin and board identifiers, publishing status, and organic performance or analytics information.

The integration also processes content and publishing information supplied by MI Party Prints, such as approved images, Pin titles, descriptions, alt text, destination links, board selections, approval status, and publishing records.

How information is used

Information is used only to operate and improve MI Party Prints' own content workflow, including preparing and publishing owner-approved organic Pinterest content, confirming publication, maintaining internal publishing records, and reviewing organic content performance.

MI Party Prints Publishing is not offered as a service for third-party Pinterest users and does not use Pinterest information for unrelated advertising or data-broker activities.

OAuth credentials and security

Pinterest authorization credentials, including access and refresh tokens when applicable, are treated as confidential. They are intended to be stored in secured infrastructure rather than in public repositories, public content, or ordinary content-management records. Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect credentials and information handled by the integration.

Sharing and sale of information

MI Party Prints does not sell Pinterest API information or personal information obtained through this integration. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the internal workflow, such as infrastructure, source-control, file-storage, or content-management providers, only as needed to provide those services and subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices. Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

Data retention

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and evaluate the MI Party Prints publishing workflow, maintain appropriate business records, and comply with applicable obligations. Temporary processing files may be removed after successful processing and durable storage of required business records.

Account authorization and access

The Pinterest account owner controls authorization of the integration through Pinterest. Authorization may be revoked using available Pinterest account or application controls. After revocation, the integration will no longer be able to use the revoked authorization to access Pinterest API information.

Third-party services

The integration may rely on third-party services to operate its internal workflow. Those services maintain their own privacy and security practices. Pinterest's handling of information is governed by Pinterest's own policies and terms.

Children's privacy

MI Party Prints Publishing is a business publishing tool and is not designed for use by children. It does not intentionally collect personal information from children through the integration.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the integration or applicable requirements change. The current version will be posted at this URL with its effective date.
