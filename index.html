MI Party Prints Publishing

MI Party Prints Publishing is an internal content-management and publishing integration used by MI Party Prints to manage its own approved organic social content.

The integration helps publish owner-approved content to the MI Party Prints Pinterest Business account and supports performance tracking for that content.
