# Theme + Product Intelligence and Opportunity Pipeline

## Goal
Decide what MI Party Prints should test, build, expand and market next while preventing a small number of early winners from crowding out discovery.

## Two opportunity types
**Theme opportunities** ask whether a theme deserves discovery, expansion or deeper investment.
**Product opportunities** ask whether a product type deserves creation/testing across one or more themes. New product formats are first-class opportunities, not subordinate notes under successful themes.

## Explore / exploit portfolio
The planning system reserves capacity for discovery. Current starting policy: at most 60% of planning capacity may be dominated by established/proven opportunities; at least 40% remains exploration. Exploration is split initially across new themes (20%), new products (15%), and experimental concepts/retests (5%).

These are portfolio planning shares, not paid-ad budgets and not guarantees that every calendar window will divide perfectly.

## Cold-start protection
No-history is not failure. A new theme/product receives a learning window and minimum organic exposure before performance alone can demote it. Compare sample sizes and maturity state whenever performance is used.

## Evidence
Keep raw inputs visible: Etsy visits/orders/revenue/conversion where authoritative; Pinterest impressions/saves/outbound clicks; catalog coverage; production effort; market signal; product maturity; and strategic fit. Do not let one opaque score hide the reason for a decision.

## Product discovery
The Opportunity Pipeline may originate new product formats independently of themes. Promising product formats can be piloted across a deliberately varied theme set: one proven theme, one mid-performing/learning theme, and one new/under-tested theme when inventory permits. This helps distinguish product-format demand from theme demand.

## Expansion logic
A successful theme can earn additional capacity for deeper products (bundles, story-driven adventures, escape-room experiences), but it cannot consume the protected exploration allocation. Likewise, a promising new product can earn broader cross-theme rollout after sufficient evidence.

## Output
Each planning cycle should produce separate established and exploration lanes, show supporting inputs/sample sizes, and explain why each opportunity is being tested. Owner approval and all no-spend rules remain unchanged.
