# Analytics & Learning Architecture

## Purpose
Connect each exact approved creative to observed organic performance, then feed evidence back into campaign planning without changing historical approved packages.

## Canonical identity
The publishing receipt/job identity is the join key. Preserve exact version, asset SHA, theme, product type, marketing job, creative concept, board and publish slot.

## Measurement windows
Capture available Pinterest organic metrics at 1, 7 and 30 days after publication. Never overwrite an earlier snapshot.

## Funnel
Impressions → Saves / Pin clicks → Outbound clicks → Etsy outcome when supported.

Pinterest metrics describe Pinterest behavior. Etsy orders/revenue are stored only when retrieved from an authoritative Etsy/source system. Do not manufacture cross-platform attribution.

## Learning
Aggregate by theme, product type, marketing job, concept, board and time slot. Report descriptive rates and sample sizes. Early data creates hypotheses, not automatic winners. The agency may use evidence to propose future campaign allocations, but owner approval rules remain unchanged.

## Theme investment
Future theme-investment analysis may combine Pinterest demand signals, Etsy traffic/orders/revenue, product coverage and production cost. Keep raw measures visible. Never let a composite score hide its inputs.

## Automation boundary
Analytics collection and calculation may run automatically. It must not trigger paid promotion, fee-generating Etsy changes, purchases, or unapproved publishing.
